# AED & first-aid kit opening checklist

Front desk, every property. The opening shift lead signs the shift board when every item is done.

## Before the lobby opens

1. **AED status light** is green. If it is red or blinking, call Facilities and post the "AED out of service" sign.
2. **Pads and battery** are in date (check the labels on the case).
3. **First-aid kit** is sealed. If the seal is broken, restock it from the supply cabinet and log what was used.
4. **Certified first-aider** is named on the shift board, with their certification expiry date.

## If no certified first-aider is on shift

- Tell the duty manager right away.
- Ask the nearest property for cover until a certified colleague arrives.
- Record it in the incident log so Learning & Development can schedule training.

## Contacts

- Learning & Development: training questions and class bookings
- Facilities: AED faults and supply restocks
