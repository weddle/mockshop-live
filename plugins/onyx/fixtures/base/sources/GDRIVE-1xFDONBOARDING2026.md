# Front Desk Onboarding Path 2026

Owner: Learning & Development. Applies to every new front desk hire, full and part time.

## Days 1–5: welcome

- Property tour and safety walk (fire exits, AED location, first-aid kit)
- Brand standards and guest greeting
- Guest privacy and data handling (e-learning, 45 minutes)

## By day 14: systems and service

- Check-in and check-out in the property system, with a buddy
- Accessibility requests: room features, service animals, mobility equipment
- Lost and found, key cards and late checkout requests

## By day 30: certifications

- Food-safety awareness (e-learning)
- **CPR/First Aid with AED**, classroom, run by Coastal Health Training
- Sign-off by the front office manager

Missed a class? Ask Learning & Development for the next open date. The training policy allows one 14-day extension when a class is cancelled.
