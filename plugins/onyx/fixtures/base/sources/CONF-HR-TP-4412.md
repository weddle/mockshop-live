# Training Policy: Required certifications by role

## Front desk

- CPR/First Aid with AED within **30 days of hire**, valid for 2 years.
- Guest privacy training within 5 days of hire, refreshed yearly.

## Housekeeping and maintenance

- Safe lifting and chemical handling within 14 days of hire.
- CPR/First Aid for shift leads.

## Extensions

A property may extend a certification deadline **once, by 14 days**, if the scheduled class is cancelled. The front office manager logs the extension and books the next open class.
